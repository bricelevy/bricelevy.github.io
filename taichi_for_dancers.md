Taichi for Dancers is a project by [Paz Moreno](https://www.pazmoreno.com/about-contact) and [Brice Levy](/apropos) for dancers and other performing and body arts professionals (theater, circus, etc.) that combines the practice of Tai Chi Chuan with contemporary dance.
<br/><br/>
It is a series of workshops exploring some of the founding principles of Tai Chi Chuan through codified Tai Chi Chuan movements and exercises, then transposed into a dance/performance context. Both solo and partner work are covered.
<br/>
Some of the principles covered :
- verticality
- intention (Yi)
- moving from the center
- relaxation/structure (fang song)
- listening skills (ting jin)
